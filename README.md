# Why chords sound good

An illustrated, listenable essay on why some notes blend and others rub: vibrations, frequency ratios, roughness, harmonics, and chords.

**Live:** https://olmo-gutierrez.github.io/why-chords-sound-good/

A single self-contained `index.html`. No build step. Turn your sound on. Every figure plays what it shows.

**Play it yourself:** https://olmo-gutierrez.github.io/why-chords-sound-good/play/ connects to a MIDI keyboard (Web MIDI: Chrome, Edge, Firefox) or your computer keys and draws the live waves of every note you hold, their sum, the chord name, ratio, harmonics and roughness.
